Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Apex Live ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered fitness coaching application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address and encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Workout data: workout type, duration, exercises, sets, reps, and notes you enter during workouts.
  • Health metrics: heart rate, calories burned, steps, and distance — read from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) with your explicit permission.
  • Voice data: audio is streamed to OpenAI's Realtime API for real-time coaching. We do not store audio recordings. Transcripts are stored to maintain conversation history.
  • Device information: device model and OS version for crash reporting via Sentry.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide real-time AI voice coaching during workouts.
  • To track your workout history, progress, and personal records.
  • To display health metrics during active workouts.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database secured with encryption at rest.
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (12 rounds) and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens with automatic refresh and rotation.
  • All API communication uses HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: processes voice audio for real-time coaching. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry: receives crash reports and performance data. Subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect: health data is read locally on your device with your permission. We sync selected metrics to your account for workout tracking.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data is retained as long as your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab. This permanently deletes all your data including workouts, metrics, conversations, and personal records.
  • You can export all your data in JSON format before deletion (GDPR compliance).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you (via the export feature).
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke health data permissions at any time through your device settings.

7. Children

Apex Live is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated through the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@apexlive.app
